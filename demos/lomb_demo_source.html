Under the Hood

Lomb scores conversations the way corpus linguists do — using the complexity–accuracy–fluency (CAF) framework from Brand & Götz (2011) and Huang & Gräf (2025), not one vague “fluency score.”

Audio is transcribed and diarized (AssemblyAI / WhisperX), then scored with Grosjean’s (1980) WPM method, vocd‑D lexical diversity (Malvern et al., 2004), and a hand-built German error taxonomy. This report shows real pipeline output; live upload processing is next.
