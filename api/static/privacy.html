Privacy policy

Version {{POLICY_VERSION}}

Lomb is an invite-only demo that analyzes recordings of German lessons and shows the speaker where their German went wrong and how to fix it. This page explains what it stores, why, where, and for how long.

What Lomb stores

  • Your Google account email and name, to sign you in and keep your lessons separate from everyone else's.
  • Recordings you upload, their transcripts, and the analysis results (the errors found, corrections and reports).
  • Records of what you agreed to on this page and when.
  • A voiceprint, only if you turn on voice recognition (see below).

How it's processed

  • Recordings, transcripts and results are stored and processed on Google Cloud, in the United States (Northern Virginia), encrypted at rest. Transcription runs on Lomb's own servers there.
  • To find errors, the text of what you said (not the audio) is sent to OpenAI's API, which processes it under OpenAI's API data-usage terms.
  • Nothing is sold, rented or shared for advertising.

Other people in your recordings

Lesson recordings usually include a tutor or conversation partner. Before uploading, you confirm that everyone audible has agreed to being recorded and to the recording being analyzed by Lomb. The whole recording is transcribed so the voices can be told apart, but only what you said is analyzed for errors. Lomb never builds or stores a voiceprint of anyone else.

How long things are kept

  • Recordings, transcripts and results: until you delete the lesson or your account. Deleting removes them from Lomb's database and storage.
  • Records of what you agreed to: until you delete your account.
  • Voiceprints: see the schedule below.

Voice recognition and voiceprints (biometric data)

What it is. If you turn on voice recognition, Lomb creates a voiceprint: a list of numbers computed from your voice by a speech model (ECAPA), describing how your voice sounds. Laws such as Illinois' Biometric Information Privacy Act treat this as a biometric identifier.

Why. Its only purpose is to recognize which voice in your future uploads is yours, so you don't have to pick yourself each time. It is never used for anything else.

How it's made. From the voice you pick as yours in a lesson, and refined each time you confirm your voice again. When matching a new lesson, every voice in it is compared with your voiceprint; only the similarity scores are kept, never anything computed from other people's voices.

Who can access it. Only Lomb's own software. It is not sold, leased, traded or otherwise profited from, and not disclosed to anyone except Google Cloud, which hosts it, or where required by law.

Retention and destruction schedule. Your voiceprint is permanently destroyed at the earliest of:

  • when you turn voice recognition off (withdraw consent),
  • when you delete your account,
  • one year after it was last used to recognize you,

and in any case no later than three years after your last interaction with Lomb.

Consent. Voice recognition is off unless you turn it on by signing a written release (typing your name). Lomb works fully without it.

Your choices

  • Delete any lesson, or your whole account, from the app at any time.
  • Turn voice recognition off at any time; your voiceprint is deleted immediately.
