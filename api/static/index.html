Lomb can remember your voice and pick you out automatically in future lessons. This creates a voiceprint, which is biometric data, so it's off unless you sign for it. You can turn it off any time.

Voice recognition: written release

What Lomb will create: a voiceprint, a list of numbers computed from your voice that describes how it sounds. It's a biometric identifier under laws such as Illinois' Biometric Information Privacy Act.

Purpose: only to recognize which voice in your future uploads is yours. Nothing else.

How: from the voice you picked as yours, refined each time you confirm your voice again. Other people's voices are compared with it but nothing about them is stored except similarity scores.

Sharing: never sold, leased, traded or otherwise profited from; not disclosed except to Google Cloud, which hosts it, or where required by law.

Retention: permanently destroyed when you turn voice recognition off, when you delete your account, or one year after it was last used, whichever comes first, and in any case within three years of your last interaction with Lomb. Full details in the privacy policy.
